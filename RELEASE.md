Fixes unchanged revision suggestions and adds two free on-device writing models.

- Gives your exact requested change priority, including changes to a character’s emotions, reactions, and actions.
- Retries once if the model copies the original passage. If it still makes no changes, reports the problem instead of saying the suggestion is ready.
- Keeps rejected unchanged answers out of the suggestion and prevents applying unchanged saved revisions.
- Adds SmolLM3 3B (about 1.8 GB download; requires 8 GB device memory) and Qwen 3 4B Instruct (about 2.3 GB; requires 12 GB device memory) to Manage Models on iPhone and iPad. Both run locally without an account or usage fees after download.
- Retains the recent iPhone safe-area and pinch-zoom fixes.
