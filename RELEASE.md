A book you can step into, with practical editing tools.

- New light and dark palettes: ivory, teal, and copper by day; midnight blue, sea glass, and gold by night.
- CODEX and WRITE! now sit on the open book page. Pages turn toward the front for Codex, or toward your last edited section for writing, then fade into the screen. Writing restores its saved position.
- Generate the next beat with a single press. The description is optional; the AI can continue from the manuscript, beat plan, and Codex.
- Edit with AI appears beside highlighted manuscript text, including deep inside a beat, and disappears when the selection is cleared.
- Built-in offline English Spell Check offers corrections and remembers character names or invented words on your device.
- Find & Replace searches the current section or the whole book, with whole-word and case options. Formatting, beat boundaries, and earlier revisions are preserved.
- Insert page breaks at the cursor. Breaks survive saving, backups, and Word, EPUB, HTML/print, and text exports.
- The writing desk fits the screen without page scrolling or white safe-area gaps. The footer slogan is removed.
- A simpler sidebar keeps book sections and bottom options with standard icons. Codex moves to the editor tools; redundant Outline and Manuscript navigation is removed.

Verified with automated data and publisher tests, browser workflows, and an iPhone build. Simulator checks remain skipped by default.
