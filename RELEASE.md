Your manuscript, one beat at a time.

- Generate opens a chapter beat planner. Review or write the beat map, then generate each beat separately on the manuscript page. Existing prose stays in place.
- Show beats highlights editable blocks with their own directions and AI revision buttons. Hide beats returns to continuous writing. Add a blank beat or prompt the next beat directly below the manuscript.
- Select a word, sentence or paragraph and choose Edit selection with AI. Review the proposed replacement before applying it; surrounding writing and earlier versions are preserved.
- Add front matter, story sections and back matter: title page, copyright, dedication, epigraph, contents, foreword, preface, introduction, prologue, chapters, interludes, epilogue, afterword, acknowledgments, appendix, glossary, bibliography, author page and custom sections. Reorder them in the manuscript; all sections export together.
- Update from manuscript reads the current writing and proposes missing Codex context, developments and character details. Review and edit additions before saving. Existing character fields and notes are preserved.
- Plot holes in the Codex checks the manuscript for possible contradictions and missing causal links. Review supporting passages and possible fixes, then track findings as open, resolved or dismissed. Findings are suggestions, not established story facts.
- Every button has an action icon. Settings now uses a recognizable gear, models a chip, and the bookshelf a shelf symbol.
- Beat maps, AI writing attempts and revisions are saved in each book’s Drafts. Generation can be stopped while keeping its output. Concurrent manuscript changes block stale AI replacements.

Includes phone-sized interaction tests for beat planning, individual generation, selected-text editing, cancellation, failed-save recovery, section exports and reviewed Codex updates. AI quality and availability depend on your chosen model and device.
