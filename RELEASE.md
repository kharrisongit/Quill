More control over every word.

- Every story beat now receives editable default instructions for clear prose, fewer clichés and repetitions, concrete story progress, and consistent locations and actions. Customize or restore the prompt in Settings. The latest manuscript ending stays verbatim in the generation context, and beat plans identify already-written events.
- The floating selection button is now Revise. Select a sentence or passage for Rephrase, or a single word for Synonyms. Review alternatives from your chosen writing model in a pop-up and tap one to replace just that selection.
- Automatic offline English spell checking underlines unrecognized words in red as you type. Tap an underlined word for suggested corrections or remember a character name. Turn live checking off in writer preferences and use manual Spell Check whenever you are ready.
- Find & Replace stays above the manuscript. Previous/next arrows visit and highlight individual matches, including across book sections. Replace one at a time by default, or enable the Replace all checkbox. Formatting, beat boundaries, and earlier revisions are preserved.
- Adjust writing text from 14 to 32 px using Text size in the editor tools. Your preference survives closing and reopening Quill.
- Generated beat suggestions now have a much larger, resizable text area. Highlighted beat text boxes are roomier too.

Includes focused replacement, live spelling, cross-section navigation, persistence, and model suggestion checks. Simulator checks remain skipped by default.
